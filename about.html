About AssignEase

AssignEase is a Virtual Classroom and Assignment Management System designed to simplify assignment submission, evaluation, plagiarism detection, and student performance monitoring.

The system helps faculty manage assignments efficiently and allows students to track their academic progress using analytics such as similarity score, submission behavior, and performance trends.

AssignEase also includes prediction logic that estimates student risk level and expected performance based on historical submission data.
